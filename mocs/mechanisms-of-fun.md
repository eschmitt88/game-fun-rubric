# Mechanisms of fun

**Framing.** The companion to [[evidence-and-measurement]]: this map is
organized by *what produces the experience*, layered from the second-to-
second loop outward to meaning. Each layer corresponds to a rubric
dimension in `docs/rubric.md`.

## Layer 0 — The loop (rubric G1, G2)
- [[skill-atoms]] · [[loops-and-arcs]] — the atomic learn-and-repeat unit;
  "strip the arcs, is a fun game left in the loops?"
- [[meaningful-decisions]] — agency + stakes; between blind guess and solved
  (Meier, Burgun, Kumari, CORGIS decision-making factor).
- [[juice-as-orthogonal-to-core-loop]] — feedback can be toggled to test
  the loop; a shipped juice-free game still underperforms (Kao).

## Layer 1 — Learning (dimension 1)
- [[fun-as-pattern-learning]] — Koster / Cook / Malone converge; fun is the
  reward for closing a pattern, boredom follows mastery.
- [[intrinsic-motivation-challenge-fantasy-curiosity]] — Malone's 1981
  triad; the ancestor of dimensions 5, 6, 7.

## Layer 2 — Challenge and failure (dimension 3)
- [[flow-challenge-skill-balance]] — necessary, not sufficient: balanced and
  boring are indistinguishable on flow self-report (Klarkowski).
- [[multidimensional-challenge-taxonomy]] — cognitive / emotional /
  performative / decision-making, near-independent (CORGIS).
- [[player-driven-dynamic-difficulty]] — adaptation inside the mechanic
  beats a settings menu (Chen); DDA has no pooled enjoyment effect.
- [[failure-and-difficulty]] — punishment taxonomy; deserved failure
  (Juul p<.016); disengagement ladder (Ballou).
- [[need-frustration-and-expectation-violation]] — frustration is the
  expectation–event delta → rubric 8.6.
- [[immersion-not-purely-positive]] — engagement co-occurs with anxiety.

## Layer 3 — Feel and feedback (dimension 4)
- [[game-feel-and-juice]] — inverted-U dose–response; music is the one
  pooled-significant lever; goal legibility beats score and bounce.
- [[feedback-coherence-vs-legibility]] — juice must direct attention, not
  divide it; quantitative trade-off still open.
- [[intuitive-controls-price-of-admission]] — controls are a gate, not a
  contributor (Ryan 2006 mediation).

## Layer 4 — Curiosity and emergence (dimensions 5, 6)
- [[information-gap-curiosity]] — tolerance tracks confidence in closing
  the gap, not its size; content vs configuration gaps.
- [[systemic-emergence]] — dynamics produce aesthetics (MDA).

## Layer 5 — Emotion and meaning (dimension 7)
- [[four-keys-to-fun]] · [[mda-framework]] — the two designer taxonomies of
  aesthetic outcome.
- [[negative-emotion-positive-experience]] — designed loss is enjoyed and
  appreciated.
- [[hedonic-enjoyment-vs-eudaimonic-appreciation]] — story feeds
  appreciation on a track that never crosses enjoyment (ADR 0004).
- [[euphoric-ease-solitary-play]] — single-player's free enjoyment floor.

## Layer 6 — Why play at all (round 3)
- [[games-as-art-of-agency]] · [[striving-play-vs-achievement-play]] ·
  [[disposable-ends]] · [[layered-agency]] — Nguyen: the medium is agency;
  arbitrary goals motivate as disposable ends; grounds 2.6 and G2.
- [[reward-schedule-taxonomy]] · [[fun-vs-compulsion-boundary]] — the
  operant machinery behind 3.5 and why it cannot judge itself.
- [[input-output-randomness-timing]] — randomness after the choice is
  fine; before it, it reads as unfair.
- [[tutorial-onboarding-design]] — value scales with discoverability.
- [[instrument-reuse-beyond-original-scope]] — GameFlow's 15-year drift;
  a warning for this rubric.

## Design-move catalogue
- [[design-lenses-catalog]] — Deterding's heuristic cards cross-indexed by
  skill-atom element × motive; the practical bridge from these mechanisms
  to rubric rows 1.2, 2.x, 4.2, 6.3, 8.1.

  outcome uncertainty) or two.
- Toys vs tools (autotelic vs instrumental systems) — no concept yet.
- Good randomness: Burgun's rejection vs Malone's endorsement; no
  controlled study in the graph.
