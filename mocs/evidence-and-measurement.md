# Evidence & measurement of fun

**Framing.** Most of what designers believe about fun is consensus, not
measurement. This map organizes the sources by *how* they know.

## Layer 1 — Controlled and pooled evidence (E1)
- [[design-evidence-quality]] — caroux2023player (meta-analysis, 70 studies:
  music g=.60 is the only significant factor); malone1981toward (Breakout and
  Darts ablations); juul2013art (self-attribution p<.016).

## Layer 2 — Validated instruments (E2)
- [[player-experience-measurement]] — PENS (ryan2006motivational), PXI
  (vandenabeele2020development), GameFlow expert review
  (sweetser2005gameflow), facial coding (lazzaro2004why).
- [[functional-vs-psychosocial-consequences]] — PXI's mediation model; the
  basis of ADR 0003.
- [[need-satisfaction-sdt-pens]] — competence > autonomy > relatedness as
  predictors; autonomy used inconsistently (tyack2020self).

## Layer 3 — Who is being measured
- [[player-motivation-profiles]] — Yee/Quantic Foundry, Bartle, Big Five.
- [[player-demographic-motivation-variance]] — age/gender swings large
  enough to change weights.

## Open problems
- No source supplies per-dimension importance weights.
- Inter-rater reliability of any expert-review rubric is unmeasured.
- Fun vs retention vs meaning are conflated in most measures.
