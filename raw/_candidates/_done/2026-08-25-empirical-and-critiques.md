Round 2 targets what the canon lacks: controlled experiments on juice,
challenge and failure; validated instruments; grounded theories of
uncertainty and need frustration; hedonic-vs-eudaimonic distinctions; and
retention telemetry (to test whether "fun" and "keeps playing" diverge).

## 1. The Effects of Juiciness in an Action RPG

- url: https://people.csail.mit.edu/dkao/
- type: paper
- summary: Kao (2020, Entertainment Computing, n=3,018) finds both no juice and extreme juice reduce play time, experience, motivation and performance vs medium/high — an inverted U.
- reason: The only large-N controlled test of juice; directly bears on 4.2 and the unsourced juice-vs-legibility gap.

## 2. Good Game Feel: An Empirically Grounded Framework for Juicy Design

- url: https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/view/936
- type: paper
- summary: Hicks, Dickinson, Holopainen & Gerling (DiGRA 2018) derive a juiciness framework from a developer survey, fixing the term's vague definition.
- reason: Gives dimension 4 a peer-reviewed construct definition rather than a talk.

## 3. Measuring perceived challenge in digital games (CORGIS)

- url: https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/23517/
- type: paper
- summary: Denisova, Cairns, Guckelsberger & Zendle (2020) validate a 30-item, 4-factor scale (cognitive, performative, emotional, decision-making) across 1,390 players.
- reason: Splits "challenge" into four kinds — likely the fix for why pooled difficulty effects are null (caroux2023player).

## 4. Manipulating Leaderboards to Induce Player Experience

- url: https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/manipulating-leaderboards-to-induce-player-experience/
- type: paper
- summary: Bowey, Birk & Mandryk (CHI PLAY 2015, N=155) experimentally induce success vs failure perception and show it raises competence, autonomy, presence, enjoyment.
- reason: Causal evidence that *perceived* success drives need satisfaction — relevant to 1.3/3.3 and the failure-attribution finding.

## 5. Operationalising and Measuring Flow in Video Games

- url: https://dblp.org/rec/conf/ozchi/KlarkowskiJWSP15
- type: paper
- summary: Klarkowski et al. (OzCHI 2015) build levels meant to induce flow/overload/boredom and find self-report flow scales fail to separate them cleanly.
- reason: The best empirical critique of flow-as-fun; bears on dimension 3's evidence tier.

## 6. The Role of Uncertainty in Moment-to-Moment Player Motivation

- url: https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/149801/
- type: paper
- summary: Kumari, Deterding & Freeman (CHI PLAY 2019) grounded theory of seven engaging uncertainty types linked to curiosity and competence motives.
- reason: Empirical successor to Malone's uncertainty mechanisms and Costikyan; feeds 5.2.

## 7. Integrating Curiosity and Uncertainty in Game Design

- url: https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/view/793
- type: paper
- summary: To, Ali, Kaufman & Hammer (DiGRA/FDG 2016) link five curiosity types to information-gap uncertainty, tested via playtests.
- reason: Operationalizes Loewenstein information gaps for 6.3.

## 8. Video Games as Meaningful Entertainment Experiences

- url: https://digitalcommons.butler.edu/ccom_papers/145/
- type: paper
- summary: Oliver et al. (2016) find enjoyment tracks gameplay + competence/autonomy while appreciation tracks story + insight/relatedness.
- reason: Empirically separates hedonic fun from eudaimonic meaning — decides whether 7.5 belongs in a *fun* rubric.

## 9. Negative Emotion, Positive Experience? Emotionally Moving Moments in Digital Games

- url: https://edoc.unibas.ch/43791/
- type: paper
- summary: Bopp, Mekler & Opwis (CHI 2016, 121 players) find sadness and other negative emotions are often enjoyed and appreciated.
- reason: Evidence for 7.2 emotional range including negative valence.

## 10. miniPXI: Eleven-Item Measure of the Player Experience Inventory

- url: https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/minipxi-development-and-validation-of-an-eleven-item-measure-of-t/
- type: paper
- summary: Haider et al. (2022) validate an 11-item PXI short form across 628 players.
- reason: The practical instrument to pair with rubric scoring in playtests (How-to-use step 4).

## 11. A Grounded Theory of Psychological Need Frustration in Video Games

- url: https://nickballou.com/publication/2022-ballou-deterding-need-frus/
- type: paper
- summary: Ballou & Deterding (2023) show how need-frustrating events (failure, coercion, loneliness) reshape expectations and drive disengagement.
- reason: The frustration side of SDT the rubric currently ignores; may justify a "need-frustration" subtractor.

## 12. Measuring and defining the experience of immersion in games (IEQ)

- url: https://pure.york.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/measuring-and-defining-the-experience-of-immersion-in-games/
- type: paper
- summary: Jennett et al. (2008) develop the IEQ and show immersion is measurable subjectively and objectively and is not purely positive.
- reason: Validated construct behind 3.4; the "not purely positive" finding matters.

## 13. The Lens of Intrinsic Skill Atoms: A Method for Gameful Design

- url: https://pure.york.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/the-lens-of-intrinsic-skill-atoms-a-method-for-gameful-design/
- type: paper
- summary: Deterding (2015, HCI journal) formalizes skill atoms and design lenses as a method.
- reason: Peer-reviewed formalization of Cook's skill atoms (1.2).

## 14. GDC 2012: Sid Meier on games as sets of interesting decisions

- url: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gdc-2012-sid-meier-on-how-to-see-games-as-sets-of-interesting-decisions
- type: talk
- summary: Meier revisits "a game is a series of interesting decisions" and what makes a decision interesting.
- reason: The primary source behind G2, currently cited secondhand.

## 15. Engines of Play: How Player Motivation Changes Over Time

- url: https://archive.org/details/GDC2016VandenBerghe
- type: talk
- summary: VandenBerghe (GDC 2016) merges Big Five, 5 Domains of Play and SDT into a model of motivation shifting from first taste to long-term play.
- reason: The OCEAN alternative for S1 that Koster points to; adds a time dimension to profiles.

## 16. This Feels Like a Complete Waste of Time (Deterding, "The Joys of Absence")

- url: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/this-feels-like-a-complete-waste-of-time
- type: post
- summary: Coverage of Deterding's FDG 2015 argument that solitary play's joy partly comes from freedom from everyday emotional self-regulation.
- reason: A single-player-specific theory of fun the rubric has no row for.

## 17. Uncertainty in Games

- url: https://openlibrary.org/books/OL26420411M/Uncertainty_in_games
- type: book
- summary: Costikyan (MIT Press 2013): uncertainty (performative, analytic, narrative…) is the fundamental source of games' appeal.
- reason: Canonical uncertainty taxonomy; not fetchable — cite via Kumari 2019.

## 18. Spelunky (Boss Fight Books)

- url: https://bossfightbooks.com/products/spelunky-by-derek-yu
- type: book
- summary: Yu's account of constrained randomness, difficulty and feedback in Spelunky's design.
- reason: Designer primary source on good randomness (counter to Burgun); not fetchable.

## 19. Predicting Player Churn in the Wild

- url: https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/predicting-player-churn-in-the-wild/
- type: paper
- summary: Hadiji et al. (2014) cross-game churn model from generic telemetry.
- reason: Tests whether "fun" and "keeps playing" diverge; retention, not fun.

## 20. How Players Lose Interest in Playing a Game

- url: https://adk.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/how-players-lose-interest-in-playing-a-game-an-empirical-study-ba/
- type: paper
- summary: Bauckhage et al. (2012): total playtime follows a Weibull distribution.
- reason: Engagement decay as a baseline against which design effects would be measured.

## 21. Video game loot boxes are linked to problem gambling

- url: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0206767
- type: paper
- summary: Zendle & Cairns (2018, N=7,422) link loot-box spend to problem-gambling severity.
- reason: Marks the fun-vs-compulsion boundary the rubric deliberately excludes.
