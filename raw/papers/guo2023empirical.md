## Abstract (as retrieved, Semantic Scholar API, cross-confirmed)

The research examines player control in games through a "sense of agency"
(SoA) framework. Using interviews (n=18) and surveys (n=654), researchers
identified five clusters across two dimensions affecting player SoA
(multidimensional scaling analysis revealed a two-dimensional space
comprising five clusters). They created a 12-item questionnaire — "the Game
Sense of Agency questionnaire" — which, through exploratory and
confirmatory factor analysis, measures four factors: **Multisensory
Presentation, Feedback Reasoning, Virtual Realism, and Control Smoothness**.
377 valid responses were used in the factor-analytic portion of the
quantitative data analysis (out of 654 surveyed). The work provides an
empirically-driven model for assessing influences on player agency.
