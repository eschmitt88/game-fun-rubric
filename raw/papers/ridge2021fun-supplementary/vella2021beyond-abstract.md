## What is recoverable without the full text

From the title, abstract fragment, and secondary characterization (a
WebSearch-tool synthesis of publicly indexed commentary on the special
issue — **not a verbatim quote, treat as paraphrase-level only**):

- Vella's title stakes the paper's core move explicitly: it argues for going
  *"beyond agency"* as the master category for games' aesthetic value,
  proposing instead an aesthetics of **being** — modes of existence /
  "ontological spaces" the game affords, not reducible to what Nguyen frames
  as the sculpted agential skeleton (goals + abilities + constraints).
- Per the same secondary synthesis: Vella's paper "offered additional texture
  to Nguyen's phenomenological landscape, emphasizing the many meaningful
  non-striving actions that players can take up in certain types of games,
  such as trying on new digital skins, languid searching for the best
  in-game vistas, or acting out side quests of their own design" — i.e. value
  Vella locates in modes of dwelling/being-in-the-game-world that are not
  goal-directed striving at all, and so are not captured by an
  agency-coherence framing.
- This is corroborated structurally by Ryall's companion piece in the same
  issue (full text obtained, see `ryall2021agential.pdf`), which opens with
  the same three-role framing (creator/spectator/player) and explicitly
  credits Nguyen's "games as agential ontologies" thesis before pivoting to
  its own complication (grinding) — the issue's editors (Yorke 2021 overview,
  DOI 10.1080/00948705.2021.2007775) frame Vella and Ryall as adjacent
  contributions in the same symposium.
