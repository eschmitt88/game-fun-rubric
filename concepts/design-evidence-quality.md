# design-evidence-quality

## Definition

A standard for weighing design claims by their evidence — controlled ablation > validated instrument > large-N correlational > expert review > designer opinion — rather than by citation count or influence.

## Why it matters here

- **burgun2015why** — Pure designer opinion, zero citations.
- **caroux2023player** — 12 of 13 pooled design-factor comparisons null despite large single-study literatures.
- **cook2007chemistry** — Frames essay as testable science but performs no test.
- **hunicke2004mda** — Conceptual paper; disclaims any formula for combining aesthetics.
- **jonasson2012juice** — Live demo, no player data.
- **juul2013art** — p<0.016 self-blame→higher rating vs p=0.06 near-null vs uncited Falstein.
- **koster2012theory** — '87%/215%' claims uncited.
- **lazzaro2004why** — ≥3-of-4 claim asserted without data; disclaims significance.
- **malone1981toward** — Mixes weak survey with two strong controlled ablations (Breakout, Darts).
- **sweetser2005gameflow** — Validation is confirmation not prediction; no inter-rater data.
- **tyack2020self** — 82.73% of 110 CHI/CHI PLAY papers cite SDT only descriptively.
- **yee2015handy** — High-N, non-peer-reviewed, self-selected, correlational.
