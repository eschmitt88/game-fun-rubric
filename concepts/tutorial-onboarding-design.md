# tutorial-onboarding-design

## Definition

Tutorial/onboarding choices (presence, context-sensitivity, forced practice, on-demand help) as an empirically testable design lever whose value scales with mechanic discoverability.

## Why it matters here

- **andersen2012impact** — E1: tutorials helped only in Foldit (+29% time, +75% progress); null or harmful in genre-typical games (on-demand help −12% levels); forced practice never helped.
