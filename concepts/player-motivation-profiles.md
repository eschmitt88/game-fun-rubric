# player-motivation-profiles

## Definition

Typologies of why different players play (Bartle, Yee/Quantic Foundry 12-factor, Big Five); the same feature can reverse preference across segments.

## Why it matters here

- **chen2007flow** — Differently-shaped flow zones per player segment; single curve alienates both ends.
- **koster2012theory** — Retracts ad-hoc models in favor of Big Five.
- **malone1981toward** — Darts sex×condition interaction: same feature, reversed subgroup preference.
- **ryan2006motivational** — Study 4 compares PENS vs Yee's typology; achievement uncorrelated with autonomy.
- **yee2015handy** — Primary empirical anchor; closes player-type-variance gap.

- **meier2012interesting** — Informal seven-archetype taxonomy, folk corroboration of S1.
- **vandenberghe2016engines** — 5 Domains ↔ Big Five as second typology for S1.
