# immersion-not-purely-positive

## Definition

Immersion co-occurs with negative affect and state anxiety, not only pleasant experience (positive affect ρ=.71, but anxiety rises under demanding pacing p=.001).

## Why it matters here

- **jennett2008measuring** — High engagement scores do not imply a pleasant experience — caution for 3.2, 3.4.

- **denisova2015adaptation** — Immersion as sole DV.
