# intuitive-controls-price-of-admission

## Definition

Intuitive controls are a necessary-but-not-sufficient gate: their effect on enjoyment is fully mediated by the competence and autonomy they enable, so they subtract fun when absent but do not add it.

## Why it matters here

- **ryan2006motivational** — IC raw correlation drops to ns once autonomy/competence entered; anchors Dim 8 subtractor framing.
- **sweetser2005gameflow** — Several criteria are usability heuristics, not enjoyment drivers.
