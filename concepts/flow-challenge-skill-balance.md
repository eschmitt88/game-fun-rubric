# flow-challenge-skill-balance

## Definition

Enjoyable engagement arises when challenge tracks skill, bounded by boredom and anxiety (Csikszentmihalyi); games extend this with flow zones per player and irregular difficulty waves.

## Why it matters here

- **burgun2015why** — Decision-making zone as strategic analog of flow channel.
- **caroux2023player** — Null difficulty effects test coarse main effects, not skill-matching interaction.
- **chen2007flow** — Flow Zone bounded by boredom/anxiety; primary anchor for Dim 3.
- **juul2013art** — Falstein: irregular wave rather than smooth channel.
- **koster2012theory** — Sawtooth of mastery spikes within (not identical to) the flow channel.
- **sweetser2005gameflow** — Eight flow elements as enjoyment model.
- **vandenabeele2020development** — PXI Challenge construct operationalizes 3.1.

- **denisova2020measuring** — Challenge side operationalized as multi-factor, not a single dial.
- **haider2022minipxi** — Challenge single item SIR .66.
- **jennett2008measuring** — Immersion distinct from and broader than flow (Myst IV).
- **klarkowski2015operationalising** — Boredom and matched-challenge conditions statistically indistinguishable on FSS-2 flow; only overload separated.
- **meier2012interesting** — Decision pacing: too many complex decisions → out of control; too slow → bored.
