# euphoric-ease-solitary-play

## Definition

Freedom from effortful emotion self-monitoring (Goffman's interaction tension) is felt as a positive experience only in solitary/anonymous play; elsewhere it is an invisible hygiene factor.

## Why it matters here

- **deterding2015joys** — Empirical reason solitary play is a distinct design space, not multiplayer minus social.
