# feedback-coherence-vs-legibility

## Definition

Juice must reinforce state legibility (coherence, focus of attention, non-overwhelm) rather than compete with it.

## Why it matters here

- **hicks2018good** — Names the 4.2↔4.4 trade-off qualitatively; not resolved quantitatively.

- **hicks2019juicy** — Visceral action-tied feedback works, generic doesn't; legibility not tested.
- **kelly2014dont** — Diegetic/contextual incoherence half: dust on non-dusty surfaces, elastic tweens on rock, gradients on pixel art, floating shadows.
