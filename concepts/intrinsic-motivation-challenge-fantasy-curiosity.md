# intrinsic-motivation-challenge-fantasy-curiosity

## Definition

Malone's tripartite theory: intrinsically motivating activities derive appeal from challenge (goals with uncertain outcome), fantasy, and curiosity (sensory + cognitive).

## Why it matters here

- **malone1981toward** — Underlies dims 5 and 6.

- **deterding2015lens** — Curiosity lenses operationalize Malone.
- **kumari2019role** — Curiosity linked to all seven uncertainty types; Content vs Configuration sub-typology.
- **to2016integrating** — Extends Malone's single curiosity construct into five types.

- **abuhamdeh2012importance** — Challenge→enjoyment moderated by intrinsic motivation × goal-directedness.
- **tang2025designing** — N=19 qualitative study of six curiosity types (incl. a novel Future Rewards Maximization Curiosity) via treasure-chest interviews; independently converges on Malone's curiosity construct decaying fast (~3-5 exposures) and needing pacing to sustain across a runtime.
