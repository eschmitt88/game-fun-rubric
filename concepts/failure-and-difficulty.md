# failure-and-difficulty

## Definition

Failure and its punishment (energy, life, termination, setback) are designed enjoyment mechanisms; players who attribute failure to themselves enjoy games more, while pooled difficulty effects on enjoyment are null.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — No pooled effect of difficulty (g=-.12) or DDA (g=.19) on enjoyment.
- **juul2013art** — Anchors 1.3, 3.2, 3.3, 8.4.
