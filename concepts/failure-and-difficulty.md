# failure-and-difficulty

## Definition

Failure and its punishment (energy, life, termination, setback) are designed enjoyment mechanisms; players who attribute failure to themselves enjoy games more, while pooled difficulty effects on enjoyment are null.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — No pooled effect of difficulty (g=-.12) or DDA (g=.19) on enjoyment.
- **juul2013art** — Anchors 1.3, 3.2, 3.3, 8.4.

- **ballou2023just** — Competence-frustration categories (stagnation, unfair situations, meaningless actions); disengagement ladder rush→adapt→disengage→quit.
- **bowey2015manipulating** — Induced failure lowered enjoyment/affect.
- **denisova2020measuring** — Distinguishes objective difficulty from felt challenge.
- **kumari2019role** — Result uncertainty inverted-U.
- **to2016integrating** — Confidence-vs-gap-size mechanism for 3.2.

- **abuhamdeh2012importance** — Close games beat blowouts; closeness ≠ raw difficulty.
- **denisova2015adaptation** — Third DDA data point: positive on immersion (η²=.156).
- **zhang2021effect** — Attribution mechanism shared with Juul.
- **martinez2024playing** — Introduces "access difficulty": inaccessibility reframed as a player-relative form of difficulty, existing between player and game, not all of it desirable — see [[accessibility-as-gate-on-joy]].
