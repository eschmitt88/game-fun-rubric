# need-satisfaction-sdt-pens

## Definition

Self-Determination Theory's claim that in-game satisfaction of autonomy, competence and relatedness predicts enjoyment and continued play, measured via PENS.

## Why it matters here

- **ryan2006motivational** — Founding empirical source for PENS; competence strongest predictor across 4 studies.
- **tyack2020self** — Meta-audit shows autonomy is operationalized inconsistently (choice-availability vs volitional).
- **vandenabeele2020development** — Mastery↔Competence r=.884 convergent validity.
