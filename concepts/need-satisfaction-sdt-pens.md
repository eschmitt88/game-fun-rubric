# need-satisfaction-sdt-pens

## Definition

Self-Determination Theory's claim that in-game satisfaction of autonomy, competence and relatedness predicts enjoyment and continued play, measured via PENS.

## Why it matters here

- **ryan2006motivational** — Founding empirical source for PENS; competence strongest predictor across 4 studies.
- **tyack2020self** — Meta-audit shows autonomy is operationalized inconsistently (choice-availability vs volitional).
- **vandenabeele2020development** — Mastery↔Competence r=.884 convergent validity.

- **ballou2023just** — Need frustration is empirically distinct from absence of satisfaction.
- **bopp2016negative** — PENS autonomy/competence predict enjoyment (β=.25/.22).
- **bowey2015manipulating** — Competence/autonomy self-report moved by fake outcome framing alone.
- **deterding2015joys** — 'Freedom' language bridges to SDT autonomy (2.5).
- **deterding2015lens** — Motive tags are SDT-shaped.
- **haider2022minipxi** — Concurrent validity vs PENS.
- **kumari2019role** — Competence/autonomy secondary links.
- **oliver2016video** — Replicates competence/autonomy→enjoyment (β=.26/.21), N=512.
- **vandenberghe2016engines** — Taste decays, SDT needs sustain long-term engagement (E4/E5).

- **ballou2024basic** — BANGS: newest validated games-specific instrument (5 studies, N=1,246).
- **rigby2007rethinking** — Trade-press companion to ryan2006motivational.
- **johannes2021video** — Largest telemetry-linked sample (N=3,274, EA+Nintendo): PENS autonomy/relatedness/extrinsic motivation predict well-being independent of objective play time, no play-time×PENS interaction.
- **kao2024how** — Pre-registered SEM, N=1,699: directly tests and confirms SDT's "granular competence feedback" claim — success-dependent amplified feedback → competence (β=.45***) → enjoyment (β=.26***), fully mediated (no direct success-dependence→enjoyment path). Also finds enjoyment and voluntary (behavioural) engagement are *not* significantly correlated in either model — a caution against treating self-report enjoyment as a stand-in for actual continued play.
- **gomezmaureira2021level** — Bayesian factorial, N=254: first in-game test (per the authors' own literature claim) of SDT's classic overjustification prediction — assured monetary compensation independently reduced exploratory behaviour (spatial entropy, distance from path/destination) on top of an explicit-goal effect, and raised valence specifically among compensated participants while waiting, read by the authors as relief at task boundedness rather than a distinct hedonic reward effect. Confounded with recruitment platform (assured-compensation participants were MTurk/Prolific by platform norm, not independently randomised), so treat as suggestive, not a clean isolated test — but it is a novel extrinsic-reward-crowds-out-intrinsic-exploration data point this concept lacked, worth cross-reading against 2.5 (self-directed play) and 5.3/6.4 (whether in-fiction rewards for exploration risk the same crowding-out this paper found for a real-money incentive).
