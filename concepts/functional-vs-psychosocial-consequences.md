# functional-vs-psychosocial-consequences

## Definition

PXI's validated two-tier model: functional consequences of design (control, challenge, feedback, goals, aesthetics) → psychosocial consequences (meaning, immersion, mastery, curiosity, autonomy) → enjoyment, with partial mediation.
