# player-reflection-depth

## Definition

Reflection on a game experience is not binary but graded (Fleck &
Fitzpatrick's 5 levels: non-reflective description → reflective description
→ dialogical → transformative → critical), and depth reached correlates
with rated emotional-challenge intensity (Kruskal-Wallis χ²(4,N=53)=13.108,
p<.011) — the gap concentrates at the reflective-description→dialogical
boundary, and autonomy-implicating + negatively-valenced design patterns
are most prominent at the deepest levels.

## Why it matters here

- **cuerdo2024exploring** — E2 quantitative link (emotional challenge →
  reflection depth) plus qualitative design-pattern mapping; extends
  7.2/7.5's afterglow/meaning framing with a behaviorally-graded construct
  rather than a single appreciation rating.
