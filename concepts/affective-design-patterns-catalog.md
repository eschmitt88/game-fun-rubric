# affective-design-patterns-catalog

## Definition

A named catalog (~20 patterns; Dormann et al., Bjork & Holopainen,
Lankoski & Björk) of concrete design moves for inducing emotional/social-
emotional engagement — e.g. Emotional Decision-Making, Empowerment,
Sacrificial Action, Identification, Consequences of Long Ago Actions,
Avatar Display of Human Frailty, Negative/Uncomfortable Emotions,
Sympathy for Victims, The Traumatized Avatar. Autonomy-implicating
patterns (Emotional Decision-Making, Empowerment, Consequences of Long Ago
Actions) and negatively-valenced patterns cluster at the deepest levels of
[[player-reflection-depth]].
