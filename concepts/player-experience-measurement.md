# player-experience-measurement

## Definition

Instruments and methods for quantifying subjective player experience — PENS, IMI, PXI, GameFlow expert review, facial coding, attribution-coded open responses.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — 60% of 70 studies used ad-hoc enjoyment questionnaires.
- **hunicke2004mda** — 8-aesthetics taxonomy as early directed vocabulary for fun.
- **juul2013art** — Attribution-theory-coded open responses as playtest pattern.
- **lazzaro2004why** — Facial/verbal coding + contextual inquiry, N=30 players, 45h video.
- **ryan2006motivational** — Introduces and validates PENS subscales.
- **sweetser2005gameflow** — Element×criteria 1–5 expert-review instrument; N=2 author-rated validation.
- **tyack2020self** — PENS and IMI each used in 40% of sample; open validity questions.
- **vandenabeele2020development** — PXI canonical validated instrument.
- **yee2015handy** — EFA + hierarchical clustering, cross-region replication.

- **ballou2023just** — Existing need-frustration questionnaires lack content validity for games.
- **bopp2016negative** — Multi-construct validated measurement in one study.
- **bowey2015manipulating** — Leaderboard rigging as embedded PX induction method.
- **denisova2020measuring** — CORGIS validated N=394 EFA, N=987 CFA.
- **deterding2015joys** — Episodic interviews + grounded theory coding.
- **haider2022minipxi** — 11-item validated PXI short form for playtests.
- **hicks2018good** — 17-respondent survey + affinity diagramming, no IRR.
- **jennett2008measuring** — IEQ 31-item, PCA N=244; 2035 citations.
- **kao2020effects** — Instruments unconfirmed (abstract only).
- **klarkowski2015operationalising** — FSS-2 control/merging subscales read high even when trivially easy.
- **oliver2016video** — Single-item enjoyment/appreciation; 4-factor PENS + insight.

- **abuhamdeh2012importance** — Objective game-generated challenge measures instead of self-report.
- **ballou2024basic** — Only instrument in graph with a frustration subscale; explains 8.4% of logged Xbox playtime.
- **denisova2015adaptation** — IEQ Challenge subscale null despite manipulation — converges with Klarkowski.
- **hicks2019juicy** — PENS + PXI + AttrakDiff2 side by side.
- **nacke2008flow** — EMG valence + GSR arousal discriminated flow from boredom; GEQ Flow/Immersion self-report did not.
- **rigby2007rethinking** — PENS beats fun ratings — vendor-asserted.
- **sweetser2020gameflow** — Positions GameFlow vs PXI/PENS/GEQ/IEQ; two unrelated 'GEQ's exist.
