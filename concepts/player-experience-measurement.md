# player-experience-measurement

## Definition

Instruments and methods for quantifying subjective player experience — PENS, IMI, PXI, GameFlow expert review, facial coding, attribution-coded open responses.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — 60% of 70 studies used ad-hoc enjoyment questionnaires.
- **hunicke2004mda** — 8-aesthetics taxonomy as early directed vocabulary for fun.
- **juul2013art** — Attribution-theory-coded open responses as playtest pattern.
- **lazzaro2004why** — Facial/verbal coding + contextual inquiry, N=30 players, 45h video.
- **ryan2006motivational** — Introduces and validates PENS subscales.
- **sweetser2005gameflow** — Element×criteria 1–5 expert-review instrument; N=2 author-rated validation.
- **tyack2020self** — PENS and IMI each used in 40% of sample; open validity questions.
- **vandenabeele2020development** — PXI canonical validated instrument.
- **yee2015handy** — EFA + hierarchical clustering, cross-region replication.
