# multidimensional-challenge-taxonomy

## Definition

Challenge separates into Cognitive, Emotional, Performative and Decision-Making components that are weakly or negatively correlated (PERF vs DM r=−.21).

## Why it matters here

- **denisova2020measuring** — Adds per-type nuance to dimension 3; corroborates 1.1 breadth.
- **cuerdo2024exploring** — Uses the CORGIS Emotional-challenge subscale
  (only) as the quantitative instrument linking emotional challenge to
  [[player-reflection-depth]]; first reuse of CORGIS beyond its own
  validation paper found in this graph.
- **flint2023comparing** — N=1,101, the first direct empirical test of
  CORGIS against a second, independently developed instrument (the Video
  Game Demand Scale, VGDS: cognitive/emotional/controller/exertional/
  social demand). Both a 5-factor "complete overlap" and 9-factor
  "no overlap" CFA model were rejected; an EFA instead resolves to
  **seven dimensions** — VGDS's five demand factors intact, plus CORGIS's
  Performative and Decision-Making challenge surviving as genuinely
  distinct. CORGIS's own Cognitive and Emotional challenge factors do
  *not* survive separately from VGDS's same-named demand factors — a
  caution that half of CORGIS's four-factor structure may be measuring
  general cognitive/emotional demand rather than a challenge-specific
  construct, while the other half (performative, decision-making) is now
  cross-instrument-corroborated as real and distinct. (Abstract-only
  capture — see `literature/papers/flint2023comparing.md`.)
