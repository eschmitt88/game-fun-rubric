# reward-schedule-taxonomy

## Definition

Fixed/variable ratio and interval schedules plus chain, extinction, behavioural contrast and avoidance, each with a predictable player-activity signature.

## Why it matters here

- **hopson2001behavioral** — Mechanism vocabulary for 3.5 and for sharp reward drops (3.2, 8.6).
- **tang2025designing** — Names and evidences "discovery fatigue": excessive reward-container density/frequency produces diminishing engagement via diminishing marginal utility (Etemadi et al. 2023) and habituation (Hall & Rodríguez 2020), independent of and additive to reward-difficulty mismatch — the *why dense schedules specifically fatigue* mechanism this concept lacked. N=19 qualitative (treasure chests, Assassin's Creed), E3.
