# fun-as-pattern-learning

## Definition

Fun is the reward signal for successfully learning a pattern or skill; boredom follows once the pattern is mastered or is unlearnable (Koster; Cook; Malone's cognitive curiosity).

## Why it matters here

- **cook2007chemistry** — Player-model axiom: pleasure from acquiring skills.
- **koster2012theory** — Grounds Dim 1.
- **malone1981toward** — Mechanism for 6.3, parallel to Koster.
- **juul2002open** — Mechanism for *why* pattern space can be deep/broad
  from few rules: "a small number of rules that combine and yield large
  numbers of game variations, which the players then design strategies for
  dealing with." Its rule-interaction / combination / emergent-strategies
  taxonomy gives 1.1 a way to distinguish shallow from deep pattern space.
