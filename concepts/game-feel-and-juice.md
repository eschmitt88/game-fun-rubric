# game-feel-and-juice

## Definition

Real-time control responsiveness plus layered, cheap, redundant feedback (screenshake, particles, squash-and-stretch, sound, music) that makes input feel satisfying.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — Music presence the ONE significant factor (g=.60, p=.01); sound effects alone ns (g=.26).
- **jonasson2012juice** — Canonical demo: screenshake, particles, squash-stretch, tweening, sound atop unchanged mechanics.
- **malone1981toward** — Breakout regression: bricks breaking β=.77 > score β=.32 > bounce β=.30; feedback elements not interchangeable.
