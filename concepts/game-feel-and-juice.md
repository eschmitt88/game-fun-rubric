# game-feel-and-juice

## Definition

Real-time control responsiveness plus layered, cheap, redundant feedback (screenshake, particles, squash-and-stretch, sound, music) that makes input feel satisfying.

## Why it matters here

- **caroux2023player** — Music presence the ONE significant factor (g=.60, p=.01); sound effects alone ns (g=.26).
- **jonasson2012juice** — Canonical demo: screenshake, particles, squash-stretch, tweening, sound atop unchanged mechanics.
- **malone1981toward** — Breakout regression: bricks breaking β=.77 > score β=.32 > bounce β=.30; feedback elements not interchangeable.

- **hicks2018good** — Developer-grounded taxonomy: Game Characteristics / Game State / Direct Feedback.
- **kao2020effects** — N=3,018 ablation None/Medium/High/Extreme: inverted-U on play time, experience, motivation, performance.
- **meier2012interesting** — Acknowledgment feedback ('at least a sound effect') distinct from diagnostic feedback.

- **hicks2019juicy** — First controlled test: juice raises appeal, curiosity, immersion in every game; never moves objective performance; competence only when juice is tied to the competence-defining action (Quake 3).
- **kelly2014dont** — Counter-talk to jonasson2012juice.
