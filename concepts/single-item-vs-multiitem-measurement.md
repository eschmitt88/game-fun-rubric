# single-item-vs-multiitem-measurement

## Definition

Single items are faster but lose reliability for complex constructs and under delayed recall.

## Why it matters here

- **haider2022minipxi** — Any rubric-companion survey must collect functional items immediately post-play.

- **ballou2024basic** — 78→53→18 item pruning; 3 items per subscale.

- **ballou2025perceived** — "Gaming life fit" is a 5-item formative average (not a validated multi-item latent scale) that still outpredicts a fully objective, session-level, 140k+-hour playtime measure by an order of magnitude — a data point that a short, deliberately-unvalidated multi-item self-report can beat a rich behavioral log for capturing subjective value, while their own single-item well-being measures (Cantril ladder, VAS affect) were the outcomes, not the predictor, in that comparison.
