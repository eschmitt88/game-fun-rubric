**Context.** Caroux & Pujol 2023 found 12/13 pooled design factors null;
Tyack & Mekler 2020 found 83% of SDT citations in games HCI are decorative.
Designer frameworks (Koster, Cook, Burgun, Lazzaro's ≥3-of-4 claim) are
influential but untested.

**Decision.** Tiers: E1 controlled/meta-analytic; E2 validated instrument or
large-N correlational; E3 peer-reviewed expert review / small-N observation;
E4 primary practitioner theory; E5 uncited opinion. A criterion may not cite
"SDT"/"flow" generically — it names the construct and the source
(`concepts/design-evidence-quality.md`).

**Consequences.** Readers can see which rows are load-bearing (1.3, 3.3,
4.5, 5.2, 7.3) and which are consensus. Weight changes require at least E2.
