title: "Dimension 7 is scored on a separate story track and its meaning payload is not weighted as fun"
date: "2026-08-25"
status: accepted
---

**Context.** ADR 0003 gated all psychosocial dimensions behind the
functional floor. Oliver et al. 2016 (N=512, SEM) show two non-crossing
tracks: gameplay → competence/autonomy → enjoyment, and story →
relatedness/insight → appreciation. Bopp et al. 2016 show negative-valence
narrative peaks are enjoyed *and* appreciated. PXI Meaning and both studies
measure in-session appreciation, not afterglow.

**Decision.** Dimension 7 keeps its 10% weight for the *fun* it delivers
(fantasy, emotional range, intrinsic fantasy, expression) but is reported on
its own track: a low gameplay-functional floor does not automatically
invalidate a 7 score, and a high 7 cannot rescue a failed floor. 7.5 is
labelled as measuring appreciation. Designed negative peaks are an explicit
top anchor of 7.2. Also adopted from round 2: new criterion 8.6 expectation
calibration (Ballou & Deterding 2023), per-type challenge calibration in 3.1
(CORGIS), and the step-4 measurement protocol (behavioural + affect +
immediate post-play, no single-item proxies).

**Consequences.** A story-led game is scored honestly for fun without being
penalised for routing its value through appreciation; raters stop mis-scoring
permanent narrative loss as a 3.2/8.4 failure.
