**Context.** ADR 0005 required corroborating or dropping 2.6 by v0.6.
Round 4: the philosophy-of-sport special issue affirms coherent agency
with boundary conditions (Ridge: needs a playful stance; Ryall: can
degrade into grind) — Vella's "beyond agency" is the one open challenge;
CHI 2021 interview data show mechanical/customization freedom lifts felt
agency even in linear narratives; operational-logics and
emergence/progression theory supply independent framings. Separately,
three telemetry studies show playtime tracks neither wellbeing nor
perceived value, and kao2024how (pre-registered N=1,699) found curiosity
the strongest predictor of enjoyment and the only predictor of
free-choice playtime, with amplified juice reducing competence via
impeded outcome binding.

**Decision.**
1. **2.6 stays**, wording unchanged, evidence annotation upgraded to
   E4-multi + E3 empirical; Vella logged as an open challenge; boundary
   conditions folded into the 3.5 cross-read.
2. New rubric section **"Fun vs retention"**: playtime is a manipulation
   check, never the score; playtest deliverable = need satisfaction +
   perceived value + rubric scores.
3. **Weights still unchanged**: kao2024how is recorded as the first
   relative-importance signal (curiosity > competence); dimension 6 is
   raised only if replicated in a second game.
4. The full Sweetser-2020-style wording pass moves to v0.6, done together
   with a worksheet regeneration.

**Consequences.** 2.6's single-source risk is retired; the rubric now has
an explicit doctrine separating fun from retention; the first
evidence-based weight change has a pre-registered trigger condition
instead of judgment.
