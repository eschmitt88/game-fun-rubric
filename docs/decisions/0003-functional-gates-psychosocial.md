**Context.** PXI (Vanden Abeele et al. 2020, N=529) validates a two-tier
model — functional consequences → psychosocial consequences → enjoyment,
partial mediation. Ryan et al. 2006 show intuitive controls act only
through competence/autonomy.

**Decision.** The rubric reports functional and psychosocial subtotals
separately. A functional average below 2.0 marks psychosocial scores as
unreliable; the two hard gates G1/G2 remain absolute caps.

**Consequences.** A beautiful, meaningful game with bad controls is scored
as "fix the floor first", matching the empirical mediation structure rather
than a flat weighted sum. Revisit if round-2 sources (Kao 2020, CORGIS)
contradict the mediation pattern.
